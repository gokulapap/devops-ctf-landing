About DevOpsCTF

DevOpsCTF is a hands-on DevOps training platform built around Capture The Flag challenges. Most DevOps training is theory-heavy with multiple-choice tests, while real-world DevOps demands practical debugging and troubleshooting.

Our platform provisions isolated, ephemeral cloud environments on demand, so engineers can practice diagnosing and fixing production-grade incidents safely. Each challenge is modeled after real outages and infrastructure failures DevOps engineers encounter daily.

DevOpsCTF serves individual engineers sharpening their skills, teams preparing for on-call, and organizations upskilling DevOps talent. Zero setup — sign in and start solving in seconds.
