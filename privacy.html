Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 September 2026

This policy explains what personal data the DevOpsCTF platform collects, why we collect it, and what we do with it.

1. What we collect

  • Account details you provide when you register — your name and email address.
  • Progress data — which challenges you have attempted, completed, and how long you took.
  • Technical logs that any web service produces: IP address, browser type, and the pages requested.

2. Why we collect it

To run the platform, to keep your progress between sessions, to show leaderboards where you have chosen to appear, and to diagnose faults. We do not use your data for advertising.

3. Sandbox environments

Challenges run in isolated environments created for your session. Commands you run and files you create inside a sandbox exist only for the life of that session and are destroyed when it ends. We do not retain their contents.

4. Who else sees it

We do not sell or rent personal data. We share it only with the infrastructure providers that host the platform, and only so far as they need it to provide that hosting. They process it on our instructions and for no other purpose.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep account and progress data for as long as your account exists. Ask us to delete your account and we will remove it, along with the progress attached to it. Technical logs are discarded on a rolling basis.

6. Cookies

This site sets no tracking or advertising cookies. The platform sets only the cookies needed to keep you signed in.

7. Your rights

You may ask what personal data we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Write to the address below and we will respond.

8. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top will change with it. Continued use after a change means the updated policy applies.
